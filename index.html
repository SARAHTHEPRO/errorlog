Common questions

  • How do I play? Drag your finger to draw a path that guides the glowing node to the goal without touching the walls.
  • What are coins for? Coins buy power-ups in the shop to help you clear tougher mazes.
  • I bought coins but didn't get them. Force-close and reopen the app; if they're still missing, email us and we'll sort it out.
  • How do I restore my purchases? Open the Shop and tap "Restore Purchases."

We typically reply within 1–2 days.
